About Our School

Seven decades of educational legacy

Our Story

Established in 1955, this school was formerly known as Mithila High English School. Today it is recognised as +2 Government Mithila High School, Balaur, affiliated to BSEB, Patna.

It is a co-educational institution offering classes from 9 to 12. Our mission is to prepare every student for the future with knowledge, discipline, and strong values.
